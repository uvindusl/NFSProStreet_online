# Code of conduct

*Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1 (CC BY 4.0).*

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

* Be respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
* Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Critique code, not people.
* Assume good faith; ask before assuming malice.
* Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise, and learn from them.
* Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behaviour

* Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
* Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
* Public or private harassment.
* Publishing someone's private information (address, email, etc.) without
  explicit permission.
* Encouraging or assisting piracy of the game or of Microsoft's DLLs. This is
  also a project rule: bring your own copy.
* Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions,
chat — and when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer through the contact in the
commit history, privately. Reports are reviewed promptly and fairly.
Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may temporarily or
permanently ban any contributor for behaviour they deem inappropriate,
threatening, offensive or harmful. Maintainers who do not follow this code in
good faith may face consequences determined by the project's other leaders.
